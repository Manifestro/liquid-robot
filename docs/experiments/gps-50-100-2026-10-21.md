## Setup and evaluation protocol

- The map and physics were unchanged. The `gps_50_100` profile places goals 50–100 m ahead along Z. Actual straight-line GPS distance can be slightly greater on the winding road.
- Each episode starts with a known north-facing orientation. Heading is estimated from GPS course and the gyroscope. Sensors are ideal, without calibrated noise or latency.
- Physics runs at 50 Hz and commands at 10 Hz. Episodes have a 240 s timeout and a 1 m goal radius.
- The preliminary transfer test without training used odd seeds 71001–71099: 48/50 successes and two collisions. These worlds are separate from the final test set.
- Validation uses 12 odd seeds 72001–72023, approximately every 10000 actions. Checkpoints are ranked by success, then collisions, then return.
- The final test uses 50 odd seeds 73001–73099, shared by the baseline, selected and latest models. Test results were not used to select the checkpoint. Training worlds use even seeds.
- PPO continued from the best 30–50 m checkpoint with a learning rate of 1e-4 and a fresh optimizer. Other parameters and the observation format were unchanged.

Training completed exactly **100000 new actions**, 391 PPO updates and 2894 optimizer steps. Training episodes: 181/184 successes and three collisions. Training, baseline evaluation, periodic validation and final evaluations took **69.02 minutes**, excluding the preliminary transfer test.
